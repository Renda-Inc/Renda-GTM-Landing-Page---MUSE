# Refine Business Carousel and Build the Alfie Page

## Scope

- Refine the Business Banking “One connected system” carousel into a wider editorial image experience.
- Build one continuous Alfie landing page using the existing Renda design language.
- Route the five Alfie navigation/footer links to that same page and its relevant sections.

## Business Banking carousel

- Increase the active image card to the established Careers image width and Culture & Values height.
- Keep the title and supporting copy over the image at the top-left with a restrained dark readability overlay.
- Move the Bank / Spend / Manage / Understand labels into a separate stage navigator within the section.
- Preserve four-second auto-rotation, drag interaction, and progress dots.
- Use an offset perspective/coverflow composition so the preceding and following cards remain partially visible.
- Adapt dimensions and interaction for touch devices without text overlap.

## Alfie page

- Replace the current Alfie Platform screen with one cohesive Renda-branded story flowing through:
  1. What Alfie is
  2. Connect / Understand / Reason / Act
  3. Individuals
  4. Business
  5. Enterprise & Government
  6. Alfie Agents
  7. Final contact call to action
- Reuse the existing navigation, footer, typography, semantic colors, spacing, imagery, corner treatment, and reveal motion.
- Use editorial imagery, restrained interaction, natural example questions, conceptual system connections, and progressively more institutional visual treatment as the page advances.
- Keep content concise and responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

## Links and validation

- Point How Alfie Works, Alfie for Individuals, Alfie for Business, Alfie for Enterprise & Government, and Alfie Agents to the same Alfie page with section anchors.
- Update both desktop/mobile navigation and footer while keeping their visual design unchanged.
- Verify the Business carousel interactions, Alfie anchor navigation, desktop/mobile rendering, runtime console, and production build status.
